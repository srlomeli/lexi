Lexi's Word Game

A relaxing 5-letter word puzzle with a friendly helper. No timer, no pressure.

How to start

Open Chrome, Edge, or Safari and go to:

lexi.salvadorlomeli.com

A new game starts right away—no sign-up or password needed.

Scan to play

How to play

  1. Guess any 5-letter word. Type on your keyboard, tap the large letters on screen, or press Voice mode / Speak to say or spell it out (like "C, R, A, N, E").
  2. Check the clues on each tile. Every tile shows a color, a symbol, and a word:
A ✓ Right
✓ Right
Right letter, in the right spot.
B ● In word
● In word
In the word, in a different spot.
C ✕ Not in
✕ Not in
Not anywhere in the word.
  1. You have 6 tries. Words Lexi doesn't know never cost a guess. Stuck? Press I give up any time and Lexi will kindly tell you the word.
Helpful features built in:
  • Why this letter? Tap any scored tile on the board to read or hear why it got that clue.
  • Voice mode & Read aloud: Hear Lexi's replies out loud and talk back hands-free.
  • Comfort settings: Extra-large text, no-color or dark high-contrast styles, an A-to-Z keyboard, and a private Letter notebook.
