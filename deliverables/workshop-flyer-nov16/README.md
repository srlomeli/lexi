## Flyer content

- **Title:** Lexi's Word Game
- **One line:** A relaxing word puzzle. Find the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries,
  with a friendly helper. No timer, no pressure.
- **How to start:** Go to **https://lexi.salvadorlomeli.com** in Chrome, Edge, or Safari,
  or scan the QR code above (`qr-code.svg`). A new game starts right away with no sign-up.
- **How to play:**
  1. Type, tap, or say a 5-letter word (or spell it out, like *"C, R, A, N, E"*).
  2. Each letter shows **✓ Right** (right spot), **● In word** (different spot), or **✕ Not in** (not in the word).
  3. Words Lexi doesn't know never cost a guess. Stuck? Press **I give up** and Lexi will kindly tell you the word.
- **Helpful extras:** **Why this letter?** tile explanations, hands-free **Voice mode** and read-aloud, **Extra large** text, no-color and high-contrast dark styles, an **A-to-Z** keyboard option, and a private **Letter notebook**.
