      1. Lexi picks a secret 5-letter word. You have 6 guesses.
      2. Type a word on your keyboard or tap the letters under the board, then press Guess. You can also talk to Lexi with Voice mode or the chat.
      3. Every letter gets one of three results:

        Select any colored letter on the board to hear why it got that result.

        Words Lexi doesn't know never use up a guess. There is no timer.

        Stuck? Press I give up and Lexi will kindly tell you the word.

        Keyboard: type letters anywhere, Backspace to delete, Enter to guess. On the board, use the arrow keys to move between letters.
